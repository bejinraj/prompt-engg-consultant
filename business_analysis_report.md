# 3. Why This Bottleneck Was Selected

The key reason is **breadth of impact**.

The metrics show three major business problems:

1. **Profitability problem:** margins are down 15%.
2. **Operational problem:** warehouses are over-stocked and inventory is tracked manually.
3. **Customer/revenue problem:** shipping takes 5–7 days, while conversion is only 1.2% and abandonment is 82%.

Inventory management directly connects the **profitability and operational problems**, and it contributes to the fulfillment problem.

By contrast, focusing only on cart abandonment or declining foot traffic would address important symptoms but would not address the underlying inventory/cost problem.

**Manual inventory tracking**

Poor inventory planning and allocation  
↓  
**Over-stocked warehouses**  
↓

At the same time:

**Poor inventory visibility + inefficient fulfillment**  
↓  
**5–7 day shipping**  
↓  
Poor customer experience  
↓  
**82% cart abandonment / 1.2% conversion**

Therefore, inventory management is the strongest single bottleneck to investigate first during the **PINPOINT** stage.
